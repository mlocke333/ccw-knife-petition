Support a Limited CCW Exception for Automatic Knives

California residents: Add your name in support of a narrow bill that would allow valid Concealed Carry Weapons (CCW) permit holders to carry switchblades and other automatic knives under the same conditions that already apply to their license.

What the proposal does:
Creates a limited exception to Penal Code § 21510 for holders of a valid California CCW license. It does not legalize automatic knives for the general public. All existing location restrictions (schools, public buildings, etc.) remain in place. No new licensing system is required.

Sign in Support

By signing, you are a California resident expressing support for this narrow, common-sense measure. Your name and contact information will be shared with California legislators and relevant advocacy organizations.

This is a grassroots petition only. It is not an official legislative comment form. Signatures will be compiled and shared with members of the California Legislature and supporting organizations (CRPA, Knife Rights, etc.).
